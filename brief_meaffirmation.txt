BRAND: MeAffirmation. Free web app at meaffirmation.netlify.app.
WHAT IT IS: A private place to speak out loud. Record up to 10 minutes (a "riff") of affirmations, dreams, good news or declarations, then either keep it privately to listen back, or release it anonymously into "the void". No likes, no comments, no followers, no names.
WHY IT EXISTS: Move in silence. Not everyone is happy for you, so you can't share every dream. Sometimes you get great news and there's no one to call. Sometimes you just need to say it out loud to yourself, to reconfirm who you're becoming.
ANGLES: move in silence; wins with nobody to tell; saying it out loud vs thinking it; riffing instead of reading scripted affirmations; prompts for what to say; morning declarations; night gratitude; voice notes to your future self; reaffirming after rejection; anonymity and zero performance pressure; listening back on hard days.
NEW FEATURE, REMEMBER WHEN (feature it in at least 2 posts a week): the Remember When manifestation method, built into the app.
You pick a topic (home, money, love, career, body, family, travel) and tell the memory of what you want as if it has
already happened: "I remember when I got the keys to my own place...", "I remember the day I met him...", "Do you
remember when...". No one to tell it to? Tell it to MeAffirmation. It saves to your Memories so you can play your own
voice back. Angles: the Remember When method explained simply; example memories by topic; why past tense and little
details make it feel real; "no friend to do Remember When with? Do it here"; replaying your memory on hard days.
HARD RULES:
- Do not claim users can listen to other people's recordings in the void.
- No therapy or mental health claims. It is a personal practice, not treatment.
- Warm, intimate, encouraging tone. Speaking mostly to women.
